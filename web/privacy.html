Privacy Policy

Daily Newspaper is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

Last updated: April 1, 2026

Daily Newspaper (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting the privacy of our readers and subscribers. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly: Name and email address when you subscribe to our newsletter, comment on articles, submit a letter to the editor, or contact our team.

Information collected automatically: When you visit our website, we collect browsing data including pages visited, time on site, device type, browser version, IP address, and referring URLs via cookies and analytics tools.

From third parties: We may receive data from advertising partners, social media platforms (when you share our content), and reader survey providers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver the newsletter and personalized content recommendations
  • To analyze readership patterns and improve editorial coverage
  • To serve relevant advertising (see Section 4)
  • To respond to reader inquiries and feedback
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

3. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies (required for site functionality), analytics cookies (Google Analytics), and advertising cookies. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings or our cookie consent tool.

4. Advertising

Daily Newspaper is supported by advertising. We work with third-party advertising partners who may use cookies to serve targeted ads based on your browsing behavior across websites. You can opt out of interest-based advertising at aboutads.info.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@dailynewspaper.com. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Data Retention

Newsletter subscriber data is retained until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is retained for 26 months. All other personal data is retained for no longer than necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify newsletter subscribers of significant changes by email. Continued use of the website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: privacy@dailynewspaper.com
